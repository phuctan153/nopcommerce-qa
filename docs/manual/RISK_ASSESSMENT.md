# Risk Assessment

This register supports [TP-001](TEST_PLAN.md). Confirmed entries have a recorded observation, a vendor-documented condition, or an explicit scope constraint. Potential entries describe plausible failures or limitations awaiting evidence. Likelihood and impact are planning estimates.

Score likelihood and impact from 1 (Low) to 3 (High). Multiply them for priority. High is 6–9, Medium is 3–4, and Low is 1–2. High impact blocks a primary journey or invalidates its result. Medium impact limits a branch, reduces diagnostic confidence, or makes the run less repeatable.

| Risk ID | Description | Category | Evidence status | Likelihood | Impact | Priority | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Demo resets can remove test accounts or change product fixtures between checks. | Environment | Confirmed condition. The QA-002 overview cites the vendor's hourly reset policy. | High | High | High (9) | Recheck account, product, options, and stock at session start. Keep runs short and recreate disposable data after a reset. Discard results that relied on invalidated fixtures. |
| RISK-002 | Shared users or repeated test runs can reuse the same account identifier or change catalog data. | Test data | Potential. Shared use is documented. A collision has not been observed. | Medium | Medium | Medium (4) | Use run-specific identifiers and isolated browser sessions. Verify fixtures before assertions and capture the actual product and options used. |
| RISK-003 | An access challenge or intermittent failure can block the storefront and invalidate broad coverage. | Availability | Confirmed past blocker. QA-002 recorded HTTP 403 and a browser security verification page. Current access is pending. | Medium | High | High (6) | Start with a normal manual access check. Log the environment and blocker, suspend affected work, and resume from a known state when access returns. |
| RISK-004 | Configuration-dependent controls or UI updates can change locator targets and make results difficult to interpret. | UI and configuration | Potential. Storefront controls and update behavior remain unverified. | Medium | Medium | Medium (4) | Confirm controls manually on representative pages. Use stable locators in page objects and wait for the relevant state or content change. |
| RISK-005 | Slow responses, stale elements, or shared state could make later automation flaky. | Automation | Potential. No suite has been implemented or run. | Medium | High | High (6) | Keep tests independent, use a fresh WebDriver session and explicit waits, and capture failure evidence. Diagnose repeat failures before adding a check to smoke coverage. |
| RISK-006 | Storefront-only access limits verification of persisted state and diagnosis of backend causes. | Observability | Confirmed scope constraint. Admin, APIs, and database inspection are excluded. | High | Medium | High (6) | Verify observable behavior through the storefront. Document gaps and avoid inferring persistence or root cause from UI evidence alone. |
| RISK-007 | Password recovery may depend on outbound mail, a controlled inbox, and a reset link that remains valid. | External dependency | Potential. Delivery, expiry, and reuse behavior are unverified. | Medium | Medium | Medium (4) | Separate recovery form feedback from delivery and reset checks. Use a disposable account and controlled inbox. Mark downstream coverage blocked if delivery is unavailable. |
| RISK-008 | Public-demo limits prevent post-submit confirmation coverage. Available payment options or shipping eligibility may also prevent reaching order review. | Checkout | Confirmed scope constraint for order submission. The last reachable step remains unverified. | High | High | High (9) | Use a shippable item and synthetic data. Stop before real payment details or final placement. Record the last reachable step and reserve completion checks for an authorized sandbox. |
| RISK-009 | Registration from one session may be mistaken for the demo's current or universal activation policy. | Coverage assumptions | Potential. QA-002 contains a single user-reported observation. | Medium | Medium | Medium (4) | Attribute that observation to its session. Confirm required fields, validation, and the active registration path before fixing detailed expectations. |
